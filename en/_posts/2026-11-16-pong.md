**Pong** is one of the first successful arcade video games. It was released in 1972, published by Atari and designed by Allan Alcorn.

It simulates table tennis in two dimensions: each player moves a paddle, a vertical bar, to hit the ball back. Whoever misses loses the point.

**Genre:** arcade / sports
